2. Toplanan Veriler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This page explains how personal data collected for the Metria waitlist is processed. Metria is a product currently in development; this policy covers only the waitlist registration data.

1. Data Controller

Data controller: Alican Kuklacı — alican.kuklaci@gmail.com

2. Data Collected

  • Email address — required for waitlist registration
  • Child's age range — for content planning (expecting, 0–6 mo, 6–12 mo, 1–2 yr, 2–4 yr, 4–6 yr)
  • Country — for localisation (Türkiye, Germany, Austria, Netherlands, US, Other)
  • Language preference — your UI language (TR / EN)
  • Price variant — which A/B price variant you were shown
  • Anonymous session events — page view, price click, and signup (not linked to your identity)

3. Purpose and Legal Basis

  • Notifying you at launch and sending the early-access invite — consent
  • Analysing price preferences (anonymised) — legitimate interest

4. Data Sharing

Your data is never sold or used for advertising. Technical infrastructure (database) is provided by Supabase (Dublin, EU), which operates under GDPR with SCCs where applicable for transfers outside the EU/EEA.

5. Retention

Your data is kept until: (a) you request deletion, (b) 12 months if the product is not launched, or (c) 24 months after product launch — whichever comes first and absent any overriding legal obligation.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Right to access, rectify, and erase your data
  • Right to object and restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise your rights: alican.kuklaci@gmail.com

7. Cookies and Local Storage

This site does not use cookies. The browser's localStorage is used to store your language preference, session ID, and A/B variant assignment. This data lives only on your device and is never sent to the server.
